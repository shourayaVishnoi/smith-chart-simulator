Graphing playground

Type one expression per line. Letters other than x, y, r, t and θ become sliders. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to reset. Hover a curve to trace it; roots, turning points and crossings snap.

Try one

Write y = …, x = …, r = … in θ, (x, y) for a point, (f(t), g(t)) for a curve, or any equation in x and y. Implicit multiplication works: 2x, a sin bx, sin^2 x.

Choose a question type

Pick a calculus question and type the function the way you would write it: x^3 - 2x - 5, sin(2x), e^(-x) cos x, sqrt(x), |x|. Limits accept pi/2 and similar. The walkthrough solves it step by step, and every number is listed further down.

Keys: ← → to step, Space to play or pause, R to restart

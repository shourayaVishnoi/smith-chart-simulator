Transformation playground

A 2×2 matrix A = [a b; c d] moves every point of the plane. Watch the grid, the unit square (its area is |det A|) and the images of î and ĵ. Dashed green lines are eigenvector directions, which A only stretches. Drag the white dot to see v and Av.
